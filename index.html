Work Styles Sorter

A short activity to help you notice what kinds of work you might enjoy, and what to explore next.

How this works

You will read 18 short statements about work activities. For each one, choose how you feel. It takes about 5 minutes.

At the end, you will see which kinds of work match your answers most, along with examples of jobs to explore.

This is a starting point, not a test. There are no right or wrong answers, and your results do not decide your future. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere. You can copy or print your results at the end.
